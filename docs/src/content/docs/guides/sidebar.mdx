Your sidebar is yours: the order of its sections, what it hides, your favorites, the numbers beside rows and sections you add yourself. Nobody else sees your choices, and each workspace keeps its own.

## Add to your sidebar

Anything you open often can sit in your sidebar, in **Favorites** or in a section of your own.

- **From a row.** Right-click a row in the sidebar, or use its **⋯** button, and choose **Add to sidebar**. Pick **Favorites**, one of your sections, or **New section**.
- **From a page.** A page's **⋯** menu has **Add to sidebar** too, for pages, databases and canvases.
- **From a list.** Beside the title of an inbox folder, a team inbox or a view, the bookmark button adds it to your sidebar.

Everything happens in place, in a menu or a small popup. You never leave what you are doing for Settings.

## Sections of your own

A section of your own holds rows of anything in the workspace: inbox folders, teams, views, mail boxes, accounts and folders, pages, databases, canvases and whole teamspaces. Each row shows its live number, as it does in its own section.

- **Add a section.** Hover the sidebar and click **Add section** under the sections, or choose **New section** in any row's **Add to sidebar**.
- **Add rows.** Click **+** on the section's title. A popup lists everything the workspace offers, with a search. A row already in another of your sections moves to this one.
- **Arrange.** Drag rows to reorder them, or into another of your sections. The section's **⋯** menu renames it, moves it up or down, folds it and deletes it.
- **Rename a row.** Its menu has **Rename in sidebar**, **Number**, **Move to section** and **Remove from sidebar**. The name is yours; the thing it opens keeps its own.

A section holds 60 rows, and your sections 300 in all. Mail rows show only while the workspace has Mail.

## Customize sidebar

**Customize sidebar**, in any row's menu, lists every section in order, the built-in ones and your own. Drag them, or use the arrows, to change the order. Hide a built-in section with its eye, and choose the number its rows show. Your own sections can be renamed and deleted there, and **Start from a Space** copies the sections of one of your [Spaces](/guides/workspaces/) with this workspace's rows.

<Callout type="tip">
Deleting a section only takes its rows out of your sidebar. The folders, views and pages they open stay as they are.
</Callout>
